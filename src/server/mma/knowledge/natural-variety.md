# Natural variety — sameness, symmetry & texture

Models drift to the average: clone items, mirrored arrangements, perfect edges and spacing, plastic-smooth surfaces, one flat colour, everything moving in step. That sameness reads as generated first. Write it out as description, inside the prompt.

1. **The brief wins.** If the user asks for symmetry, a grid, identical items or a clean flat look, give exactly that. The product, label, logo, packaging and any designed shape keep their true form; variety lives in the natural things around them.
2. **Write the trait, never the ban.** Engines do not process negation: "not symmetrical", "no identical", "avoid plastic" pull the image toward those words. For this family this guide outranks any negative list elsewhere: never send symmetry, identical, repetition, perfection, plastic or smoothness as bans, not even in an Avoid line.
3. **Think per subject, write it short.** Find the elements at risk (anything repeated: fruit, flowers, stones, leaves, cups, tiles, a crowd; natural materials; the hero surface) and give each one compact clause in its own words, beside it. About 10 to 25 words in the whole prompt. Shorthand like "irregular, different sizes, shapes and shades, imperfect, asymmetric" is the intent; translate it into what the eye would see for this subject.
4. **Only what this prompt makes or changes.** Elements kept from the user's photo are not re-described.

## Sameness → what to write

- Clones → mixed sizes, shapes, ripeness, shades, orientations.
- Mirror or dead-centre arrangement → "a loose cluster, heavier on one side, one rolled apart".
- Perfect geometry → "a hand-thrown bowl with a wavy rim", "petals of uneven length".
- Even spacing or grids → "irregular gaps, small clusters, some overlapping".
- Smooth surfaces → the real texture: "pitted peel", "linen with slubs and creases", "porous stone", "oak grain with a knot".
- One flat colour → "tonal shifts", "mottled", "sun-faded at the edges".
- Too pristine → one or two marks that belong: a stem scar, a crumb, a water ring.
- Stylized work → "hand-drawn line of varying weight", "uneven fills".
- Video in sync → "each leaf on its own rhythm", "people at different paces".

Example, "oranges on a wooden table, morning light" → "A loose pile of oranges in mixed sizes, some slightly flattened, dimpled peel shading from deep orange to yellow-green near the stems, one still carrying a leaf, on an oak table with open grain, low morning light from the left."
