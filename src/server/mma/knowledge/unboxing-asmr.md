# Unboxing & ASMR Mode — Adaptive Prompt Formula

Use this module when tactile interaction, material response and close sound are the story.

## Selection rule

- One beat = one completed action, one material response and one hero sound.
- Never paste the whole library into one prompt.
- Track packaging and product state before and after every beat.
- Do not invent packaging, tools, hands, sounds, text or product surfaces not supported by the brief.

## Prompt formulas

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Sub-mode (always choose one) | Prompt formula:** Register: [pure tactile ASMR / soft-spoken ASMR / voice-over unboxing / fast social unboxing / luxury ritual / raw parcel opening]. Keep speech, pace, camera and sound hierarchy consistent with this register.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Blueprint (always apply) | Prompt formula:** [Format, duration and shot structure]. The sequence begins with [initial packaging state] and ends with [final product state]. Audio = [speech rule], [music rule], [room tone] and one dominant close material sound per beat.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Product-state ledger (always apply) | Prompt formula:** For each beat define: start state = [exact object and packaging state]; action = [one completed physical action]; end state = [resulting state]. Removed, opened or placed items remain where they are unless visibly moved.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Action chain (always apply) | Prompt formula:** [Supporting hand] braces [object]; [active hand and fingers] apply [force] to [contact point]; [material] resists or deforms; release occurs; [object] settles. The matching sound begins exactly at visible contact and ends with natural decay.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Material sound (choose one per beat) | Prompt formula:** Hero sound: [specific contact between two materials], with [texture], [duration], [loudness] and [decay]. Keep secondary noises lower and physically linked. No generic “satisfying ASMR sound.”

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Microphone perspective (always choose one) | Prompt formula:** Microphone perspective: [extreme close / close tabletop / observer distance] positioned [relative to contact]. The hero sound dominates according to distance, while room tone and secondary contacts remain proportionate. No impossible giant taps or detached dubbed sound.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Hand anatomy and pressure (always apply when hands appear) | Prompt formula:** [Left/right] hand supports while [left/right] hand performs the action. Define [grip points and finger placement], visible tissue compression, natural cuticles and stable finger count. Resistance creates a brief slowdown before release; wrists and forearms enter from a believable origin.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Macro camera behavior (use for close detail) | Prompt formula:** Frame [contact point or product detail] with [macro or compressed perspective]. Keep focus on the active contact plane, allow one deliberate focus change only when attention moves, and keep camera motion subordinate to the hand action.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Tactile lighting (use for studio or tabletop scenes) | Prompt formula:** Use [soft directional source] to reveal [texture, pressure and deformation], with [edge reflection or negative fill] for reflective materials. Preserve coherent shadows and reflections; no animated light sweeps or omnidirectional white light.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Editing rhythm (use for montages) | Prompt formula:** Rhythm follows anticipation → resistance → release → recovery. Cut only after the action completes or its sound decays, unless a deliberate match cut continues the same direction and force.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Brand and text integrity (always apply to supplied products) | Prompt formula:** Preserve [product geometry, label, cap, hinge, nozzle and orientation]. Only [approved surfaces] may contain text. No invented logos, duplicate labels, barcodes, certifications, mirrored text or brand bleed.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Controlled imperfection (select two or three only) | Prompt formula:** Preserve [fingerprint edge / paper fibre / film dust / micro-tremor / slight crease irregularity] as sparse, stable details. Do not add grime, damage or sterile perfection unless requested.

- **Unboxing & ASMR | Conditional negative prompt | Prompt formula:** Avoid [packaging reset / teleporting product / floating hands / sound before contact / unfinished actions / rubber materials / giant dubbed taps / excessive reverb / cinematic whooshes / duplicate product / mutating fingers / random autofocus / unwanted speech or music]. Remove any negative that does not apply.

## Compact output structure

```text
Unboxing: register = [sub-mode]; start state = [state]; end state = [state]; each beat contains [one action] with [material response] and [hero sound]; hands = [support role + active role]; mic = [distance and perspective]; camera = [contact-focused behavior]; continuity = [state rules].
```

## Core default bans unless asked for by brief

- No multiple major actions inside one beat.
- No sound before visible contact.
- No packaging reset or repaired seal.
- No product teleportation or disappearing removed items.
- No giant taps, bass enhancement, reverb or stereo sweeps.
- No floating wrists, fused fingers or impossible grip.
- No music, whisper or voice-over unless the chosen sub-mode requires it.
- No hardcoded fragrance box, ribbon, film, hands, exact timings or full example.
